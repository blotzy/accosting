How to Play

Fly close to floating isles without colliding to build your streak and multiplier. Brake when needed and use the wind to your advantage.

  • Desktop: WASD or Arrow keys. Shift/Space to brake.
  • Mouse: Hold right-click to steer.
  • Mobile: Use the virtual joystick and brake button.
